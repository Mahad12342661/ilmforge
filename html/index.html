The GPU Rendering Pipeline

Follow the journey of a 3D object as it becomes the pixels displayed on your screen.

01. Vertex Processing

The GPU processes the coordinates of individual vertices and transforms them into screen space.

02. Triangle Processing

Vertices are assembled into triangles that form the surfaces of 3D objects.

03. Rasterization

The GPU determines which screen pixels are covered by each triangle.

04. Fragment Processing

The GPU calculates the colours and other properties of fragments using shaders.

05. Framebuffer Operations

The final pixel values are stored in the framebuffer and displayed on your screen.

Why Does This Matter?

GPUs are responsible for rendering the graphics we see in video games, animations, simulations and many other applications.

Understanding the rendering pipeline helps reveal how complex 3D scenes become the 2D images displayed on our screens.
